Setup

  1. Run the installer and choose the application folder.
  2. Select a model, its storage folder, and your Lineage II system folder. Complete the download and configuration.
  3. Open the translator and your game. Position the overlay; new chat is translated as it appears in the log.

In the installed launcher, open Settings to check for updates. When a newer version is available, click Install update & restart. Models and settings are kept.

Click Channels on the overlay to slide open its filter tray. Choose the chat channels you want to see—for example, Say and Party. Your selection is saved and applies immediately. Drag the top-right grip to resize the overlay.

Replies

Click a language code in the overlay to select a reply language, or a player name to address that player. Type in English and press Enter. The translation is copied to the clipboard for you to paste into the game.

Recognized universal trade listings show [--]; choose their reply language manually. Change the game folder or translation preferences in the launcher’s Settings.

Compatibility and notes

  • Chat logs have been verified with Reborn Legacy and Origins. Other clients need a supported Network.log; choosing a folder does not enable game logging.
  • The app reads existing logs. It does not access game memory, modify game files, or send messages. Server rules still apply.
  • Translations can misread slang or missing context. Known item links use an offline English item database; unknown IDs keep their original names. The Devotion clothing nickname devo stays unchanged in recognized trade messages and replies. Custom server items may differ.
  • Party recruitment descriptions are translated while their level ranges and player counts stay unchanged. Recognized web addresses keep their original spelling.
  • Updates preserve settings and downloaded models, and clean up verified obsolete program files and temporary downloads. Uninstalling removes the app and its managed models, runtime, settings, and logs.
  • Chat and replies are processed locally. Downloads and update checks contact external servers; those servers can see your IP address. Update checks include the app version, not chat text. Clipboard history or sync follows your Windows settings.
