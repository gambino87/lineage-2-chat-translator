Get started

  1. Install for your Windows account. Python is included.
  2. Choose a model, its storage folder, and your L2 system folder before downloading.
  3. Click Download & configure. After verification and cleanup, finish the installer and open the translator. Downloads require several gigabytes.

Qwen3.5 9B is the current tested choice (about 6.6 GB of model weights). A smaller Qwen2.5 3B option and an experimental Qwen3.5 4B option are available. The shared runtime and temporary download files need additional space.

On your computer

Translation runs locally after setup. The app reads your selected client’s chat log. Replies are copied for you to paste into the game. Model speed depends on your hardware and other GPU use.

Log support was observed with Reborn Legacy and Origins. Other clients need verification. Translation may misread slang or Greeklish; item names follow the source log.

The included uninstaller removes the application, its downloaded models, local runtime, settings and logs. App upgrades preserve existing downloads. Your game files remain untouched.
